# T-011: 44 px tall hit areas for short controls

## Goal
T-010 gave the small square controls 44×44 px hit areas. A re-audit at 375 px found wide controls
whose height is still under 44 px. Give each at least 44 px of tappable height without changing
how it looks, the same way T-010 did (a transparent absolutely positioned `::before`/`::after`
that grows only the short axis, only as far as 44 px, never overlapping a neighbour's area).

Measured (width × height, px):
- `.btn.sm` (e.g. Home's body weight Log, Goal; Stats' small buttons; Library's By muscle): 73-118 × 36
- `.chip` and `.chip.ichip` (Library filters): 70-98 × 32
- `.seg` buttons (Settings → Look and everywhere): 68-88 × 40
- `.card-head-main.tap-main` (Home card heads): ~285 × 39
- `.sp-back` (Settings page back link): 96 × 34

Check each rule's existing pseudo-elements first (some already use `::after`, e.g. `.tap-main`)
and pick one that is free, or extend the existing one. Rows of chips and segment tracks sit close
together: grow vertically only, never into the control beside or above.

## Context
- `frontend/src/index.css` holds all of these rules; T-010's `.iconbtn::before` rule is the model.
- `src/design-review.test.js`, `src/segmented-height.test.js` and the a11y tests must stay green.
