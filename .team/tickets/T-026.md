# T-026: Progress: steps, active minutes, resting heart rate and sleep

## Goal
PLAN.md Phase 5: the Progress (Stats) screen shows the Fitbit data from Google Health as cards,
with week, month and year ranges like its other charts. Friends without a tracker see nothing new.

- **Server** (`api/health/data.js`, `api/health/index.js`): also pull `active-minutes` with
  `dailyRollUp` (verified field names: each `rollupDataPoints[]` item has `civilStartTime` and
  `activeMinutes.activeMinutesRollupByActivityLevel[]` of `{ activityLevel: 'LIGHT'|'MODERATE'|'VIGOROUS',
  activeMinutesSum: "<string>" }`); store `activeMinutes: { day: moderate + vigorous }` (light left
  out: it is what counts as "active" in WHO terms). Keep 400 days instead of 120 (KEEP_DAYS), so a
  year range fills over time; the first backfill stays 30 days. Extend `api/test/health-google.test.js`.
- **App** (`frontend/src/views/Stats.jsx` and a new `frontend/src/components/ActivityCards.jsx`):
  an "Activity" section, only when `useGoogleHealth().info.connected` and there is data:
  - **Steps**: a bar per day (week), per week average (month, year), and the average of the range.
  - **Active minutes**: the same, with a line for the WHO's 150 minutes a week (as 150/7 a day on
    daily bars), labelled.
  - **Resting heart rate**: a line over the range, latest value and the change against the range's
    start ("down 3 bpm"), lower is shown as better in words, not colour alone.
  - **Sleep**: hours a night as bars, and the average.
  Reuse the chart components and range control Stats already uses (read Stats.jsx and its weight
  card first); colours from the tokens (`--chart-*`, `--heart-rate`), each chart with a text
  equivalent for screen readers, like the weekly training chart on Home.
- Pure helpers for the series (`frontend/src/lib/activity-series.js` + test): bucket by day/week for a
  range, averages that ignore missing days, the resting-HR change.
