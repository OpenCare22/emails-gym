# T-008: Workout screen: Finish and Discard in the ⋯ menu, Focus view by default

## Goal
Two decisions Emil made for the active workout (docs/fork/DESIGN_PLAN.md, correction 3 and step 5):
1. Finish and Discard leave the workout header and become the first two items of the ⋯ menu
   ("Finish workout" opens the existing Finish sheet; "Discard workout" uses the existing
   confirmed discard). The editor of a saved workout keeps its Save button in the header.
2. Workouts open in the Focus view (one set at a time) by default: the default of
   `workoutView` in `frontend/src/store/useStore.js` DEF becomes `'focus'`. Cards, list and
   compact stay available under ⋯ → Layout.

Then make the test suites green again by updating the upstream tests that assumed the old header
and the card view, without weakening what they protect.
