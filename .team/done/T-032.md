# T-032: Friends 5: trophies, a trophy cabinet and unlockable card backgrounds

## Goal
PLAN.md Phase 7 item 7. Needs T-031. Build on what the app already has (streak badges at
4/12/26/52/104 weeks in `frontend/src/lib/streak.js`, monthly challenges in `lib/challenges.js`,
medal art in components): trophies for group standings and personal milestones, a cabinet on each
person's profile in the group, and card backgrounds unlocked by trophies.

- Server: at each week's end (the daily tick, per group, in UTC Monday 00:00 terms is fine), record
  the weekly winners of each shared metric (rank 1, ties all win) as trophies:
  `{ id, uid, groupId, kind: 'week-<metric>', week, value }` in `DATA_DIR/social/trophies.json`.
  Personal milestones from the summary, awarded once: first workout, 10/50/100/250 workouts,
  streak 4/12/26/52 weeks, first record, strength ratios (squat 1.0/1.5/2.0, bench 0.75/1.0/1.25,
  deadlift 1.25/1.75/2.25 x body weight). Only metrics the person shares with that group count for
  standings; milestones are personal and shown only where `feed` or that metric is shared.
- `GET /api/social/trophies?groupId&uid` (member) → that person's trophies visible to this group.
- Unlockables: card backgrounds for the Friends row and the profile card (4 to 6 original SVG
  patterns in the Email's Gym style, sage/charcoal, no third-party art), each unlocked by a trophy
  count or kind ("Win any week", "12-week streak", ...). The chosen one is a setting synced in the
  person's state (`S.socialCard`), sent with the summary so friends see it.
- App: tap a row to open that friend's card: name, the chosen background, trophies as a cabinet
  (grid of medals with names and dates, locked ones greyed with how to earn them for your own card).
  A "Trophies" section in Friends for yourself with the backgrounds to pick. A small celebration
  (respecting reduced motion) the first time a trophy shows.
- Tests: weekly winners with ties, milestones once only, privacy (unshared metrics never award
  standings trophies), unlock rules, the cabinet UI.
