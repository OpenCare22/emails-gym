# T-030: Friends 3: nudges

## Goal
PLAN.md Phase 7 item 4. Needs T-029. A "Nudge" button on a friend's leaderboard row sends them a push
in one of the existing nudge tones.

- Server `POST /api/social/nudge` `{ groupId, uid }` (in `api/social/routes.js`): both must be in the
  group; the target must have `nudges` on; at most one nudge per sender per target per calendar day
  (sender's time zone) and 10 a day per sender; 429 `already-nudged` / `limit`. The push goes through
  the server's existing `sendPush(userId, payload)` (hand it to the social module from server.js), tag
  `nudge-<senderUid>`, url `#/friends`. Text: pick a line by the **target's** chosen tone
  (`reminder.tone` in their state summary; store the tone in the summary in this ticket:
  friendly | guilt | drill, `api/nudge.js toneOf`), with the sender's first name, from a short list of
  lines per tone in `api/social/nudge-lines.js` (write 4 per tone, plain, kind even in "drill", no em
  dashes, e.g. friendly: "{name} is cheering you on. Fancy a session today?").
  Never says what the target did or didn't do. No push subscription: answer 200 `{ sent: false }`.
- The leaderboard rows carry `nudged: true` when the caller already nudged that person today
  (so the button shows "Nudged" after a reload).
- App: the Nudge button on others' rows (`views/Friends.jsx`), toast "Nudge sent" / "They'll get it next
  time they open the app" / the limit messages; "Receive nudges" switch already in the group settings
  sheet (T-029) sets `/api/social/nudges`.
- Tests: limits, opt-out, membership, the tone choice, `nudged` on rows; the button states.
