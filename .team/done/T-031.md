# T-031: Friends 4: activity feed and kudos

## Goal
PLAN.md Phase 7 item 5. Needs T-030. A small feed per group of finished workouts and records, for
members who share `feed` with that group, with a one-tap kudos.

- Server: when a profile's state is saved, new finished workouts (reuse the detection in
  `api/integrations/events.js detectEvents` for `workout.finished` / `workout.pr`; import it, don't
  copy) become feed items in each group the person shares `feed` with:
  `{ id, uid, at, kind: 'workout', title: <workout name, or "a workout">, sets: n, prs: n, minutes }`.
  No weights, no exercises, nothing else from the log. Kept 30 days, at most 200 items per group, in
  `DATA_DIR/social/feed/<groupId>.json`. Turning `feed` off removes that person's items from that group.
- `GET /api/social/feed?groupId` (member) → newest first, 50, with names and `kudos: { count, mine }`.
  `POST /api/social/kudos` `{ groupId, itemId }` toggles the caller's kudos (not on your own items).
  A kudos on your item sends you a push at most once an hour ("{name} gave you kudos"), only if you
  have nudges on.
- App: a "Feed" tab next to the leaderboard on the Friends screen ("Emil finished Push Day, 2 records"),
  with relative times and a kudos button (44 px, count, pressed state with `aria-pressed`). Empty
  state: "Nothing yet. Workouts appear here when friends who share them finish one."
- Tests: item creation and privacy (no weights), sharing off removes items, kudos toggle and the
  own-item rule, the push throttle, the feed UI states.
